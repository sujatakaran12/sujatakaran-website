Selected work

From the logbook

Four projects, each in the same shape: the problem, the approach, and what changed because of it.

ENTRY 01

Marketing Measurement Framework

Challenge

Marketing performance was reported inconsistently across business units, with no shared definition of success.

Approach

Adapted Gartner's marketing metrics hierarchy into a Return-on-Objective model, giving every business unit a common KPI taxonomy tied to business outcomes.

Outcome

Replaced a patchwork of disconnected reporting with one shared source of truth, used consistently across the function.

Gartner ROO KPI Taxonomy Power BI
ENTRY 02

AI Strategy & Data Governance

Challenge

AI adoption across Marketing & Communications was scattered and ungoverned, with no shared roadmap or accountability model.

Approach

Led the function's AI transformation roadmap and governance operating model, evolving AI from tool to agent to influencer, and drove adoption through Microsoft Copilot and agentic workflows.

Outcome

Built and scored a portfolio of candidate AI agents against value and feasibility, with two live in production workflows, including a Social Media AI Agent reviewing roughly 70% of content and cutting drafting time by about half.

AI Strategy Data Governance Copilot
ENTRY 03

Webinar Performance Dashboard

Challenge

Webinar performance data (attendance, engagement, Q&A, pipeline impact) lived in disconnected tools with no live view for the team.

Approach

Built Power BI dashboards tracking live Q&A, poll and survey responses alongside attendance and pipeline data in one unified view.

Outcome

Featured in Salesforce Ben's account of Wärtsilä's webinar strategy, where webinar-influenced pipeline grew roughly 78x year over year. Insights from one session's live Q&A led to a sale worth more than €1M.
